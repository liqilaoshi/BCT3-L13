对话文本与分句朗读

点按声波图标可单独播放该句音频

谢经理

Lǐ jīnglǐ, huānyíng nín lái wǒmen gōngsī. Wǒmen kāishǐ jīntiān de tánpàn ba.

李经理，欢迎您来我们公司。我们开始今天的谈判吧。

Manager Li, welcome to our company. Let's start today's negotiation.

李经理

Wǒmen zài zuò shìchǎng diàochá shí, fāxiàn guì gōngsī de chǎnpǐn jiàgé bǐ qítā gōngsī gāo.

表我们在做市场调查时，发现贵公司的产品价格比其他公司高。

When we did market research, we found that your company's product price is higher than other companies.

谢经理

Wǒ xiǎng guì gōngsī diàochá shí, yīnggāi yě zhīdào wǒmen de chǎnpǐn shì zuì yǒumíng de, zhìliàng hěn hǎo. Zhè ge jiàgé yìdiǎnr yě bù guì.

我想贵公司调查时，应该也知道我们的产品是最有名的，质量很好。这个价格一点儿也不贵。

I think when your company investigated, you should also know that our products are the most famous and of high quality. This price is not expensive at all.

李经理

Chǎnpǐn de zhìliàng dāngrán shì zuì zhòngyào de. Dànshì zhè ge jiàgé tài gāo le, néng bu néng piányi yìdiǎnr? Rúguǒ guì gōngsī gěi wǒmen yōuhuì 10%, wǒmen zài mǎi 1000 ge.

产品的质量当然是最重要的。但是这个价格太高了，能不能便宜一点儿？如果贵公司给我们优惠 10%，我们再买 1 000 个。

Product quality is of course the most important. But this price is too high, can it be cheaper? If your company gives us a 10% discount, we will buy another 1,000 units.

谢经理

Wǒmen kǎolǜ le yíxià, rúguǒ guì gōngsī zài mǎi 1000 ge, jiàgé kěyǐ yōuhuì 10%.

我们考虑了一下，如果贵公司再买 1 000 个，价格可以优惠 10%。

We have considered it. If your company buys another 1,000 units, the price can be discounted by 10%.

李经理

Hǎo! Nà wǒmen zhè cì mǎi 7000 ge.

好！那我们这次买 7 000 个。

Great! Then we will buy 7,000 units this time.

谢经理

Xièxie! Xīwàng xià cì guì gōngsī hái huì zài xuǎnzé wǒmen de chǎnpǐn!

谢谢！希望下次贵公司还会再选择我们的产品！

Thank you! I hope your company will choose our products again next time!
