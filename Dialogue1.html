女顾客

Nǐhǎo, wǒ de dōngxi jīntiān néng sòng dào ma?

你好，我的东西今天能送到吗？

Hello, can my things be delivered today?

男商家

Zhēn bàoqiàn, jīntiān sòng huò de rén tài duō le, kěnéng sòng bù guòqù.

真抱歉，今天送货的人太多了，可能送不过去。

I'm so sorry, there are too many deliveries today, so we might not be able to deliver it.

女顾客

Shì ma? Dànshì wǒ jīntiān yíyào yòng, wǒ yìhuǐr zìjǐ qù ná ba.

是吗？但是我今天一定要用，我一会儿自己去拿吧。

Really? But I must use it today, I'll go pick it up myself in a while.

男商家

Nà tàihǎo le. Wǒ xiànzài jiù bǎ nǐ de dōngxi zhǔnbèi hǎo.

那太好了。我现在就把你的东西准备好。

That's great. I will get your things ready right now.

女顾客

Hǎo de, xièxie. Wǒ dàgài sān diǎn dào.

好的，谢谢。我大概三点到。

Okay, thank you. I will arrive around three o'clock.

男商家

Bú kèqi, zàijiàn.

不客气，再见。

You're welcome, goodbye.
